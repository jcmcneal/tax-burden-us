    FICA

    • Social Security: 6.2% on earnings up to $176,100
    • Medicare: 1.45% on all earnings
    • Additional Medicare: 0.9% on earnings over $200,000 (single) / $250,000 (MFJ)

    State Income Tax

    Brackets

    Bracket Rate Income Range
    1 4.65% $0+

    Flat rate: 4.65%

    • Standard Deduction: None
    • Personal Exemption: None

    Local Income Taxes

    No local income taxes.

    Sales Tax

    • State rate: 4.85%
    • Average local add-on: 1.42%
    • Combined average: 6.27%
    • Food taxable: Yes
    • Food taxed at reduced rate of 1.75% at state level; local rates may add.

### FICA
- **Social Security:** 6.2% on earnings up to $176,100
- **Medicare:** 1.45% on all earnings
- **Additional Medicare:** 0.9% on earnings over $200,000 (single) / $250,000 (MFJ)

### Brackets

| 2 | 3.50% | $12,500 – $50,000 |

- **Personal Exemption:** $4,500 (single) / $9,000 (MFJ)

### Local Income Taxes
No local income taxes (parishes have sales tax, not income tax).

- **State rate:** 4.45%
- **Average local add-on:** 4.70%
- **Combined average:** 9.15%
- **Food taxable:** No

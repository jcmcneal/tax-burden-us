### FICA
- **Social Security:** 6.2% on earnings up to $176,100
- **Medicare:** 1.45% on all earnings
- **Additional Medicare:** 0.9% on earnings over $200,000 (single) / $250,000 (MFJ)

---

## State Income Tax

**No state income tax on wages.**

- 7% capital gains tax on long-term gains over $270,000 (with $270k exemption)

### Local Income Taxes
No local income taxes.

---

- **Food taxable:** No

- **Social Security:** 6.2% on earnings up to $176,100

- **Additional Medicare:** 0.9% on earnings over $200,000 (single) / $250,000 (MFJ)

## Sales Tax
- **State rate:** 6.50%
- **Average local add-on:** 2.90%
- **Combined average:** 9.40%
- **Food taxable:** No
  - Groceries partially exempt (0.125% state rate).

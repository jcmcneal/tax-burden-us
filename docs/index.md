# US Tax Burden by State and Income

A comprehensive comparison of total tax burden across all 50 states + DC, broken down by filing status and income level.

## What's Included

- **Federal income tax** (2025 brackets, single and married filing jointly)
- **FICA** (Social Security + Medicare, including Additional Medicare Tax)
- **State income tax** (brackets or flat rates for each state)
- **Local income taxes** (where significant)
- **Sales tax burden** (estimated based on consumption patterns by income)
- **Property tax burden** (estimated based on home value-to-income ratios)

## How to Use

Navigate to any state from the sidebar to see:

1. Full tax bracket tables (federal + state)
2. Sales and property tax rates and estimated burdens
3. **Computed total tax burden** at 8 income points
4. Effective tax rate at each income level

## Methodology

- **Filing statuses:** Single and Married Filing Jointly
- **Income points:** 25k, 50k, 75k, 100k, 150k, 250k, 500k, 1M
- **Deductions:** Standard deduction (federal + state-specific)
- **Sales tax burden:** Estimated taxable consumption as a declining percentage of income
- **Property tax burden:** Estimated home value as a declining multiple of income
- **Local income tax:** Representative rate for the state's major metro area where applicable
